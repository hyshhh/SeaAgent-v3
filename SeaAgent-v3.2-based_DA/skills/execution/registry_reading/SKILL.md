# Sea-Video Registry Reading

1. Confirm what came back: did the lookup succeed, how many items and reference images, and is the catalogue simply empty.
2. Check where the gallery came from: matching registry references answers "which record looks like this"; matching video keyframes answers "which footage looks like this". Mixing the two inverts the conclusion.
3. Band the scores with the system's own bands; never promote a gray-zone score into a conclusion.
4. Report the top score, the grey items and the empty-catalogue fact. Never manufacture video track evidence out of a registry result.
5. Name the gaps: catalogue listed but no appearance filter run; matcher failed on arguments; coverage limited to part of the registry. An empty catalogue is not a failure — it is negative evidence the answer may rely on.
